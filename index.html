The language of markets

A glossary for
getting started.

What is a spread?+

The spread is the difference between the price at which an instrument may be bought and sold. It can vary with market conditions.

What is leverage?+

Leverage allows exposure to a position larger than the funds committed. It magnifies losses as well as gains, and you could lose your entire investment or more, depending on the product.

What is a CFD?+

A contract for difference (CFD) is a complex derivative that lets parties exchange the change in an asset's price. CFDs do not give ownership of the underlying asset and carry significant risk.
